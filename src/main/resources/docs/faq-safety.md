# Safety

Your safety matters on every trip. Uber provides several built-in safety features.

During a trip:
- Share your trip: send your live location and driver details to a trusted contact so they can follow
  along in real time until you arrive.
- Verify your ride: always check the driver's name, photo, vehicle model and licence plate against what
  the app shows before you get in.
- Emergency assistance: the in-app safety toolkit includes an emergency (SOS) button that connects you
  to local emergency services and shares your location.

After a trip:
- Every trip is tracked with GPS, and you can rate your driver and report any concern from the receipt.
- Lost an item? Use the trip receipt to contact your driver about a lost item.

If you ever feel unsafe, use the emergency button or contact local emergency services directly.
