# Ride Cancellation Policy

You can cancel a ride at any time from the app before or during the trip. To cancel, open the active
ride and tap "Cancel Ride".

Cancellation fees:
- Cancel within 2 minutes of requesting: no cancellation fee.
- Cancel more than 2 minutes after requesting, or after a driver has been matched and is on the way:
  a small cancellation fee may apply to compensate the driver for the time and distance already
  travelled toward your pickup.
- If your matched driver is running significantly late or is moving away from your pickup, you can
  cancel without a fee.

Any cancellation fee is charged to your default payment method and shown in the trip receipt. Rides
that are still in the REQUESTED state (no driver matched yet) can usually be cancelled free of charge.
